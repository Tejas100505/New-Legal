I reviewed your performance documentation regarding the regional sales director in Seattle. Because this employee filed an OSHA safety complaint three months ago regarding generator maintenance logs, an immediate termination presents high legal risk of a statutory retaliation claim under Section 11(c) of the OSH Act. To defend against such an action, we need clearer contemporaneous documentation establishing that performance deficiencies predated the protected whistleblower activity. I recommend placing him on a 30-day objective performance improvement plan first or negotiating a mutual separation agreement with a comprehensive release.
