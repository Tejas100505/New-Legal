Executive Team,

We have evaluated the legal implications under Section 1 of the Sherman Act regarding the proposed joint capacity coordination pact with Northern Gas. Because the agreement contemplates mutual notifications of uncommitted throughput prior to open-season postings, this creates an acute risk of per se illegal horizontal market allocation. I advise against executing the memorandum of understanding in its current form. If the commercial objective is operational reliability, we must restructure the arrangement through a blind third-party clearinghouse mechanism.
